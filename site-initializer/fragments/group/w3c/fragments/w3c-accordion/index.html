What is W3C?

The World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) is an international community where Member organizations, a full-time staff, and the public work together to develop Web standards.

How do specifications become standards?

Specifications move through the W3C Recommendation track: Working Draft → Candidate Recommendation → Proposed Recommendation → Recommendation.

Who can join W3C?

Organizations of any kind — large companies, small startups, universities, governments, and individuals as Invited Experts — can take part in W3C work.
